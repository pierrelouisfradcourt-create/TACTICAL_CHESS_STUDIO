# Economy Map

Tetris Guideline: Points=ClearType_base*(Level+1)+soft_drop; Single=40, Double=100, Triple=300, Tetris=1200. Lines cleared trigger level. Balance via level-based multiplier, line threshold=difficulty gate, no soft cap. Sources: https://tetris.wiki/Scoring

NES Tetris: Points=ClearType_base*(Level+1); no soft-drop bonus. Fixed 10-line threshold, gravity exponential early, plateaus level 29. Sources: http://www.world-of-nintendo.com/manuals/nes/tetris.shtml

Puyo Puyo: Points=Chain_score[N]*multipliers; Chain 1-5 exponential, 6+ linear, group+color bonuses. Versus: garbage escalation, counter reduces 1:1. Sources: https://puyonexus.com/wiki/Puyo_Puyo

V1 Economy: Minimal (FOG-3). Lines Cleared (primary), Points (flat rate, no multiplier), Gravity (constant). Proves mechanic; tuning=playtest. No level system, no difficulty knobs.
