## Observation architecturale — Tower Defense

J'ai analysé trois jeux Tower Defense comparables, reconnus pour leurs boucles de rétention solides et leur progression progressive. Voici l'architecture observée :

### **Bloons TD 6** — [Dinogame Review](https://dinogame.gg/blog/bloons-td-review/) | [Bloons Wiki](https://bloons.fandom.com/wiki/Bloons_TD_6) | [Strategy Guide](https://www.bloonswiki.com/Strategy:Bloons_TD_6)

**Assemblage architectural** : Bloons TD 6 structure la difficulté en tranches temporelles (Early: rounds 1-40 / Mid: 41-80 / Late: 81-140 / Freeplay: 140+), avec un **économie de farming** centrale — les banana farms débloquent une boucle d'investissement long terme où accumulation de monnaie prime sur placement immédiat. Chaque tour porte 3 chemins d'amélioration distincts (seulement 5 tiers sur un chemin, 2 tiers max sur un autre), créant une tension stratégique : optimiser VS explorer. Les ennemis (Bloons) héritent de propriétés immuables (camouflage, régénération, armure) qui forcent adaptation tactique par wave.

**4 boucles temporelles** :
- **Minute 1** : Dart Monkey initialisant la défense, placement exploratoire des tours selon la géométrie, cash bootstrap limité (~650$).
- **Minute 10** : Premiers upgrades (Dart→Sharp Shots), banana farms posés pour momentum économique, patrouille mid-map établie.
- **Heure 5+** : Métamorphose — late-game MOABs (gros ennemis multi-couches) imposent spécialisation (mage piercing, artillery splash), farm loops matures génèrent 5-10k$ par round, 5e tier d'upgrades débloqués (~rounds 60-80).
- **Endgame** : Freeplay (round 140+) avec Bloons randomisés, ceramic rushes, super-céramiques, cash-per-pop dégradé → gameplay devient théâtral (show off strats) plutôt que lutte de survie.

**Modes et conditions** : Campaign (1-60, victoire si BFB final cleared) + Freeplay (open-ended, défaite seulement si leaker). Endless randomization après round 140.

**Rétention identifiée** : (1) Layered progression — chaque mode débloque capacités nouvelles. (2) Farming loops reward patience strategique. (3) 3 upgrade paths par tour = réplayabilité — même tour joué différemment selon route choisie. (4) Heroes (personnages actifs) ajoutent tactilité. (5) Endless content après victoire = ludic permanente. (6) Cosmetics (pas pay-to-win selon sources) alignent monétisation.

### **Plants vs Zombies 2** — [PvZ Wiki Adventure Mode](https://plantsvszombies.fandom.com/wiki/Adventure_Mode/Strategy_guide) | [Udonis PvZ Stats](https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/plants-vs-zombies) | [Daily Tasks](https://plantsvszombies.fandom.com/wiki/Daily_Tasks)

**Assemblage architectural** : PvZ2 inverse la ressource primaire — *sun* (passively collected, not earned by kills) remplace coins. Sunflower devient générateur core, pas défense : économie d'**energy management** prime planning. Game divise en Worlds (8+ thèmes : Ancient Egypt, Pirate Seas, etc.) avec 40+ levels progressifs. Chaque level introduit zombie type nouveau (Pharaoh, Pirate, etc.) imposant counter plant specific. **Daily/Weekly events** (Timed Events, Piñata Parties, Zombosseum) forment loop de re-engagement — thème limité, 5-level climbs, rewards escaladés. Plant leveling permet stat boosts (damage, health) sans gacha : progression compressible par grinding ou spending.

**4 boucles temporelles** :
- **Minute 1** : Sunflower planted pour générer sun (25 sun timer), 1-2 basic plants (Peashooter) pour damage immédiat, sun collection tactile (tap before vanish).
- **Minute 10** : Sun flow stabilisé (~50-100 sun accumulation), mid-wave zombie types apparent, plant rotation selon counter (fire vs ice zombies, etc.), energy management (sun limits choices).
- **Heure 5+** : Progression into harder worlds (Desert, Modern Day), special level types (Last Stand = limited sun start + no regen) impose adaptation, boss encounters (Zomboss final level each world).
- **Endgame** : Post-campaign events, weekly challenges, plant leveling to +40 stats, cosmetics (costumes, variants), leaderboard PvP (Piñata score ranking).

**Modes et conditions** : Adventure (40 levels), Last Stand (no sun regen), Locked & Loaded (fixed plant roster), Survival (infinite waves). Boss = final level per world.

**Rétention identifiée** : (1) **Daily events** = forced re-engagement (Timed Events, Tasks, Piñata Parties). (2) **Plant variety** (50+ plants) + cosmetics prevent staleness. (3) **Ad monetization** (daily coin/gem watch) lower barrier vs PvP. (4) **Leveling compression** (can grind or spend) accommodates casuals. (5) **Thematic worlds** refresh visual identity per 8 levels, narrative lite but present.

### **Kingdom Rush** — [Kingdom Rush Wiki](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Bloons_TD_6) | [Endless Challenge](https://kingdomrushtd.fandom.com/wiki/Endless_Challenge) | [GameDeveloper Level Design](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/design/kingdom-rush---the-wonderful-campaign-level-design)

**Assemblage architectural** : Kingdom Rush restricts tower placement (not free canvas) — pre-positioned slots force positioning literacy (chokepoint vs coverage). 4 core towers (Archer, Mage, Artillery, Barracks) spawn melee units (Barracks innovation) creating hybrid placement ≠ pure fire. Campaign = 12-17 levels, each stage introduces enemy type (Goblins→Thieves→Ogres→Spiders) requiring adaptation. **Stars currency** (earned per level completion based on skill: 0 lives lost, no towers damaged = max stars) gates upgrades for *next* levels — past-run performance enables future-run options. Iron Challenges (veteran difficulty, no damage/losses) gate veteran cosmetics. **Endless Challenge** = waves at 2% stat scaling per spawn (HP, damage grow), 10 starting lives, no level exit.

**4 boucles temporelles** :
- **Minute 1** : First tower selection (Archer default, cheap, generalist), chokepoint identification on map, initial lane setup.
- **Minute 10** : Wave 3-4 introduces enemy type forcing counter (Mage vs armored, Barracks for melee block), tower upgrade tier 1-2, cash flow (~400-600 gold from kills).
- **Heure 5+** : Campaign mid-stage, 8+ waves, boss spawns in many levels, tower tier 3-4, star economy mature (next-level upgrades planned), special enemy synergy (simultaneous Ogre + Thief waves).
- **Endgame** : Post-campaign Endless Mode, waves infinite 2% scaling (damage math breaks around wave 150-200 unless optimized), hero leveling XP, cosmetic/achievement unlock races (Iron Challenges).

**Modes et conditions** : Campaign (12-17 levels, fixed waves, victory = clear all enemies), Endless (infinite waves, start 10 lives, defeat when lives=0), Iron Challenge (same level but zero tolerance for errors). Each level = discrete win state (all bloons dead OR enemy escapes = loss).

**Rétention identifiée** : (1) **Star-gated upgrades** (past skill gates future power). (2) **Enemy type introduction** per level (learning curve, novelty). (3) **Fixed tower slots** = mastery not spamming (depth in constraint). (4) **Endless mode** extends post-campaign. (5) **Difficulty tiers** (Normal/Veteran/Impossible) + Iron Challenges (cosmetic proof). (6) **Hero leveling** persists across campaigns. (7) **Tower synergies** (Barracks tanks, Archer picks, Mage armors) reward planning.

## Archétype commun observé

Tous trois partagent :
- **Resource economy** (sun, cash, stars) convertible en power, not raw stat purchase → decision weight
- **Progression slicing** (early/mid/late/endless) with distinct enemy archetypes per slice
- **Tower/plant variety** forcing respeccing per wave/level
- **Time management loop** : plan → place → execute → pause → replan (humans need cognitive breathing room)
- **Cosmetics + optional video ads** (retention without pay-wall)
- **Endless/freeplay post-campaign** (endgame playground)
