# CYBERSENTINEL

status: DOCUMENTED_ONLY

CyberSentinel is a future phase only.

## Purpose

- Defensive local/private audit assistant.
- Protect projects and sensitive player/payment-related data boundaries.
- Detect risky local states while coding.
- Alert for VPN inactive when required.
- Alert for suspicious repo exposure or secret-like files.
- Lightweight intrusion indicator/cutoff concept.

## Limits

- No permanent heavy monitoring by default.
- No auto-fix before HumanGate.
- No secrets printing.
- No autonomous network action.
- No RGPD claim without legal/security review.
