# AI Organization Chart V0

AI Organization Chart V0 documents the target scalable organization model for future AI-assisted control-plane work. It is documentation only. It does not create active agents, runners, profiles, registries, workflows, permissions, API calls, or autonomous execution.

HumanGate remains final authority for merge, reject, freeze, promotion, budget, and claim decisions.

See [CONTROL_PLANE_VISION_MAP_V0.md](CONTROL_PLANE_VISION_MAP_V0.md) for the macro/data/analysis/director/board/CEO/HumanDecision separation that this org chart fits inside.

## Target Pyramid

The intended authority chain is pyramidal:

1. Human Founder
2. Governance Kernel
3. CEO / Producer AI
4. CEO Office AI
5. Project Breakdown AI
6. Director Layer
7. Specialists
8. Workers / Tools

This is a pyramid, not a constellation. Authority narrows as it moves downward, and summaries compress as they move upward. Work must not fan out into a free swarm where every actor talks to every other actor, assigns itself work, or bypasses review gates.

## Layer Responsibilities

### Human Founder

The Human Founder is the final decision maker. The human decides merge, reject, freeze, promotion, budget, claim status, and whether any future active capability should exist.

### Governance Kernel

The Governance Kernel protects boundaries. It preserves HumanGate, no auto-ready, no auto-merge, no unauthorized tool permissions, no claim escalation, and no weakening of `NO_CLAIM_ALLOWED`.

### CEO / Producer AI

The CEO / Producer AI owns product intent, prioritization proposals, and high-level tradeoff framing. It does not talk to every specialist directly. It receives compressed briefs from the CEO Office and returns bounded direction.

### CEO Office AI

The CEO Office AI is the anti-noise layer. It triages inboxes, compresses reports, tracks follow-ups, routes escalation, and prepares decision briefs. It does not code, merge, mark ready, activate budgets, decide runtime behavior, escalate claims, or execute worker tasks.

### Project Breakdown AI

The Project Breakdown AI decomposes approved direction into bounded project packets. It does not self-authorize execution and does not create active workers.

### Director Layer

Directors own domains such as architecture, quality, resources, memory/evidence, security, or operations. They synthesize specialist reports and resolve domain ownership before escalating upward.

### Specialists

Specialists are narrow experts. They answer bounded questions, identify risks, and produce constrained recommendations. They do not self-assign work, merge, mark ready, launch tools, train models, spend money, or promote claims.

### Workers / Tools

Workers and tools execute bounded TaskPackets only. They report what was done, what changed, which commands ran, what passed, what failed, what was skipped, and which risks remain. They do not create their own authority.

## Communication Rules

- The CEO / Producer AI should receive decision briefs, not raw logs from every specialist.
- Directors should receive bounded specialist outputs, not uncontrolled handoffs.
- Specialists should receive scoped questions or packets, not open-ended mandates.
- Workers should receive bounded TaskPackets and return execution reports only.
- All communication should trend toward packetized records that can be audited locally.

## Explicit Non-Activation

This document does not activate agents. It does not modify the agent registry. It does not define runnable profiles. It does not add scripts, schemas, workflow permissions, GitHub automation, Codex automation, OpenAI calls, model training, benchmark logic, or runtime behavior.
