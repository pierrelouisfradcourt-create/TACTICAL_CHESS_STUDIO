# Specialist Reporting Policy V0

Specialist reporting flows through the governance chain:

- Worker -> Specialist -> Director -> CEO Office -> Producer -> Human Founder

## Reporting Rules

- Specialists may not bypass Directors except during critical escalation paths that are explicitly allowed by policy.
- Specialists should not forward raw logs upward unless explicitly requested by the receiving Director layer.
- Specialist outputs must be compressed into DirectorReport-compatible findings with bounded evidence notes and clear risk statements.
- Escalation conflicts route to the Director Layer first, then Governance Kernel when unresolved.
